## Collection Rules

- Every student fills the Excel file and submits electronic scans plus paper copies of award certificates/proofs.
- Paper copies should have student ID, name, and score type written on the back in pencil.
- Current summer collection covers materials generated from `2025-09-01` to `2026-06-30`.
- Materials generated from `2026-07-01` to `2026-08-31` are collected later after school starts.
- Summer deadline in the notice: `2026-07-05`.
- Certificates, announcements, and proof materials should have official stamp when applicable.
- Folder name: student name. Category folders should contain `身心素养`, `文艺素养`, `劳动素养`, `创新素养`; they may also include the student name and total category score.
- Each category should distinguish `基础性评价` and `成果性评价` when relevant; subfolder names may include the student name, category, and score.
- Electronic proof names should look like `项目名 xx分`; for contested items include classification, level, rank/award, and personal order.
- Final organized folder names should mirror the example: first-level category folders include student name + category + total score, and second-level proof folders include student name + category + `基础性评价证明材料`/`成果性评价证明材料` + sub-score.
- For direct submission packages, remove non-counted materials from both the Excel and proof folders unless the student wants to keep a separate review/archive copy.

## Overall Calculation

`素质能力评价成绩(100) = 身心素养(15) + 文艺素养(15) + 劳动素养(25) + 创新素养(45)`

School comprehensive score formula:

`综合评价成绩 = 学业水平(学习成绩) * 90% + 素质能力 * 10%`

Same project in the same indicator: count only the highest applicable award, not repeated awards.

## Relationship With 拓培系统/志愿学时

In this project, the拓培系统 part should be treated as志愿学时 evidence:

- Use拓培系统 records to verify volunteer hours for劳动素养基础性评价.
- Apply the comprehensive evaluation labor rule: 20 volunteer hours = 3 points; 30 volunteer hours = 5 points.
- Volunteer hours also constrain德育等级 eligibility: at least 20 volunteer hours are required to be eligible for `良`; at least 30 volunteer hours are required to be eligible for `优`.
- Do not convert拓培学分 directly into综测分数.
- Do not use拓培系统 records by themselves to classify文艺、身心、创新 items unless there is a separate proof material showing the activity type and eligibility under综测 rules.

## 身心素养

Cap: 15 = 基础性评价 9 + 成果性评价 6.

基础性评价:

- If physical fitness test is qualified: normally 9.
- If `国家学生体质健康标准` is not qualified: deduct 6, so the example uses 3.
- If not participating in required psychological assessment/screening: deduct 3.

成果性评价:

- Sports contests: only competitive events organized by school or student's college for校级/院级. Fun/check-in events do not count.
- Psychological contests: school or student's college organized events for校级/院级.
- Same type multiple awards: count the highest one.
- Rank conversion for psychology-style awards: rank 1 = first prize; ranks 2-3 = second prize; ranks 4-6 = third prize.
- When a student wants to improve this score, ask them to search for official, stamped evidence of校运会/院运会/official competitive sports awards or psychology contests such as心理主题短剧、心理科普海报、心理健康图文创作、心理班会/心理活动评比等. Do not count purely趣味、打卡、非竞技 sports events.

## 文艺素养

Cap: 15 = 基础性评价 9 + 成果性评价 6.

基础性评价:

- Approved daily participation/performance in aesthetic education activities may receive 9.
- In the 2025-2026 collection notice, this part is described as requiring no student proof and being directly assigned from daily performance. If there is no known不达标 notice, use `达标` and 9 points, subject to class/college final review.

成果性评价:

- Art performances and art contests count when within valid organizer scope.
- School/college events should be organized by the school or this college. Other colleges' internal events do not count.
- Same type multiple awards: count the highest one.
- Rank conversion: rank 1 = first prize; ranks 2-3 = second prize; ranks 4-6 = third prize.

## 劳动素养

Cap: 25 = 基础性评价 15 + 成果性评价 10.

基础性评价:

- Social work: one academic year or more, active duty, assessed qualified by college/unit: 4.
- For student roles, class committee/league branch/dorm leader roles are clearly in scope when assessed qualified. Ordinary干事 roles in offices, studios, or associations should not be assumed to score unless the college/unit confirms they meet the social-work criterion. If劳动基础 already reaches the 15-point cap, omit unneeded uncertain干事 items from the final workbook.
- Volunteer hours: 20 hours = 3; 30 hours = 5.
- Social practice: for freshmen/sophomores, two or more listed activities = 5; one or more = 3. For juniors/seniors, one or more listed activities = 5.
- Dormitory labor: full score 5; one failed dorm inspection deducts 1 from responsible person, two failures deduct 2, increasing similarly.

## Excel And Folder Finalization Notes

- The official Excel template contains sample/attention rows. For a clean one-student workbook, keep rows 1-6 as headers and place the student's official row at row 7 after removing the merged `A7:E8` sample block and later notes rows.
- Check that `A7:E7` contains sequence, class, student ID, name, and moral grade. A common failure is writing data to row 8 while `A7:E8` is still merged, which hides basic information.
- Keep final workbook reasons concise and submission-ready. Remove internal review text such as `待人工确认`, `按人工口径`, `需确认`, `不计创新`, or `据学生反馈`.
- Category folder scores should reconcile to Excel sub-scores:
  - 身心 = G + I
  - 文艺 = K + M
  - 劳动 = O + Q
  - 创新 = S + U
  - Total = V

成果性评价:

- Social work honors, social practice/volunteer outcomes, dormitory honors/culture awards, career development competitions, and military service may count.
- Same type honors at different levels: count the highest, not cumulative.
- Retired students with excellent service performance: 10.
- Team awards often depend on leader/member order; record personal role and order.

## 创新素养基础素养

Cap: 5.

Categories include innovation/entrepreneurship projects, papers, patents, innovation contests, entrepreneurship practice, and overseas academic exchange.

Innovation/entrepreneurship training projects completed and accepted:

- National: host 4, first three non-host members 0.8 each, other members 0.5.
- Provincial: host 3, first three non-host members 0.5 each, other members 0.3.
- School: host 2, first three non-host members 0.3 each, other members 0.2.
- College: host 1, first three non-host members 0.2 each, other members not counted.
- Excellent completion doubles project score.

Papers with Shandong University as first unit:

- Chinese core or equivalent: first author 5; co-first authors divide 5 by number of co-first authors; other authors not counted.
- Papers require review by college teaching guidance committee and comprehensive evaluation leading group.
- Related-person or senior-author collaborations are reference only and generally not counted.
- Publication deadline: current academic year August 31.

Patents with Shandong University as first applicant unit:

- National invention patent: first inventor transferred 5; first inventor not transferred 2.5.
- Utility model: first inventor transferred 2; not transferred 1.
- Design patent: first inventor transferred 0.5; not transferred 0.25.
- Transfer cases require amount proof and restrictions regarding relatives' companies.

Per-person limits:

- Innovation projects, papers, and patents: each category counts at most 2 items.
- Contest awards: at most 3 items; repeated project counts highest only.
- Entrepreneurship company: at most 1.
- Innovation basic score total is capped at 5.

Innovation contests:

- Use Attachment 1 for A/A-/B/C/D/E classifications and Attachment 2 for college A*/A-*/B* filings.
- Attachment 2 should be matched by the student's own college or clearly applicable college filing. In the current local file, no `低空科学与工程学院` row was found during setup; do not apply another college's A*/A-*/B* filing without manual confirmation.
- A/A* base score table applies first.
- A-/A-* = half of A/A* base score, only first 9 award members.
- B/B* = one third of A/A* base score, only first 7 award members.
- C = one fourth, only first 5 award members.
- D = one eighth, only first 3 award members.
- E = one sixteenth of highest-level award score, only first 1 member.
- If graduate students are in a mixed team and a graduate student is captain, calculate undergraduate order after removing graduate students from the team for that award year.

创业实践:

- Company must be registered in Shandong University incubator and operating normally.
- Legal representative: at least 1 year including 1 year and profitable = 4, personal share at least 30%.
- Legal representative: 6 months to under 1 year = 2, personal share at least 30%.
- Major shareholder: at least 1 year including 1 year and profitable = 1, personal share at least 10%.

Overseas academic exchange:

- Long-term school/college signed project over 90 days: up to 5.
- Short-term project 90 days or less: up to 3.

## 创新素养突破提升

Cap: 40.

Modules: research achievements, contest awards, international organization internship. Modules may add together but total cap is 40; within each module, if there are multiple items, count only the highest item.

Research achievements:

- Shandong University as first author unit.
- Student is sole author or first author.
- Journal: CSSCI excluding extended edition and supplements, or SCI/SSCI/EI journals excluding conference proceedings.
- Related to academic study.
- Publication deadline: current academic year August 31.
- Requires application, college approval, organized defense, and expert score up to 40.
- Co-first authors split the research achievement score equally.

Contest awards:

- Must be within undergraduate school-recognized and filed competition list.
- A/A* national authoritative contests related to study: individual national third prize or above; team national third prize or above with main members. Main member order should be top 3 undergraduates, with graduate-student order extension allowed, but total order must be within top 5.
- A-/A-* national contests: individual national second prize or above; team national second prize or above with same main-member rule.
- A/A*: national first = 40, second = 30, third = 10.
- A-/A-*: national first = 20, second = 10.
- Requires college review/approval and filing.

International organization internship:

- Requires proof after internship, student application, college agreement, school audit/defense.
- Domestic international organization internship at least 3 months: up to 24.
- Domestic at least 6 months: up to 32.
- Overseas at least 3 months: up to 40.
- Online internship deducts 8 from the corresponding level.
